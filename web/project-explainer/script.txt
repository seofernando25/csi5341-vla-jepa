# hook | THE QUESTION
This is a robot @brain:*brain*. Five and a half @gb:*gigabytes*, released already trained, someone else's checkpoint.
Our job was simple: make it smaller, and prove it still works.

# why | WHY SHRINK IT
Why bother? Because it runs on a benchtop, not a robot.
Shrink it by six and you can start thinking about arms, not workstations.
Compression is the easy part. The @proof:*proof* is what this is about.

# policy | THE POLICY
The policy is @vla:*{VLA-JEPA|V L A J E P A}*. Vision, language, action.
It sees a frame, reads a sentence, moves the arm.
The benchmark is @libero:*{LIBERO|Li B E R O}*: ten tabletop tasks. Bowl from the counter, onto the plate.

# success | WHAT COUNTS
What counts as success? The bowl is on the @plate:*plate*, or it isn't.
No partial credit. No style points.
A policy that looks busy and fails is still a failure.

# episode | ONE ROLLOUT
Here's a single @trial:*trial*. The simulator resets to a fixed initial state, with a fixed seed.
The policy sees one frame and one instruction, and acts, step by step, until the task is done or the clock runs out.
Every candidate sees those same states. Same @frames:*frames*, same @words:*words*, same *seeds*.

# rule | THE MEMORY RULE
Smaller means one thing here: @bytes:*bytes*. Resident memory.
Every tensor the solution keeps alive: the tensor walk, the @live:*live CUDA* bytes, the @peak:*peak CUDA* bytes.
We take the largest of the three, so hiding work in memory doesn't count as progress.

# footprint | THE WHOLE THING
Five and a half gigs is the policy resident at once. Weights, caches, every buffer the pipeline keeps open.
Shrinking it means the arithmetic still has to reach the same place.

# phases | THREE PHASES
Every claim comes with a phase attached, and every phase has a rule.
@smoke:*Smoke*: ten trials, one per task. @eight:{8/10|eight of ten} gets you promoted.
@dev:*Development*: a hundred trials. Development guides selection.
@reserved:*Reserved*: five hundred trials, fifty per task, read once, after selection is frozen. Reserved never guides selection.

# budget | AN AFTERNOON
An episode is maybe ten seconds. Five hundred of them is an afternoon on one GPU.
That's the order: smoke first, development second, reserved last. And last, only once.

# paired | SAME FIVE HUNDRED
Five hundred states, fixed in advance, fifty per task.
Every candidate runs the same five hundred, so a win isn't luck.
At a hundred trials the margin is about fifteen points. At five hundred, it's about *four*.

# audit | WHO CHECKS
Nobody marks their own homework here.
Agents write the solutions, a fixed evaluator scores them, and an auditor reads every diff looking for tricks.
Most failures aren't bad ideas. They're good ideas, measured badly.

# gate | NO BENDING
And the evaluator can't be argued with. It's fixed code.
The gate won't bend for a candidate.
If a solution fails, it gets reported as failed.

# search | THE SEARCH
The search is @rsi:*{Dream-RSI|Dream R S I}*. An agent walks a tree of compression branches, one node at a time.
A development gate decides what survives, and a policy trained on the search's own history decides where to look next.
It dreams on the history of the search, not on new rollouts.

# exploit | THE CAUGHT TRICK
Cycle three gave us a good one. A node claiming a hundred percent removal.
Its smoke score was exactly the reference's @d82:*{82|eighty two}*.
It was the reference, unchanged. Two more nodes had the same trick.
We threw all three out.

# results | WHAT WE FOUND
On development, the reference solves @d82b:*{82|eighty two}* of a hundred.
Our best cut removes more than *eighty percent* of the memory and still solves @d84:*{84|eighty four}*.
Another removes @d87:*{87|eighty seven}* percent, at eighty of a hundred. Within noise.
One caveat worth saying out loud: that best one runs about @lat:*{2.7x|two point seven times}* slower. Size was the score. Speed wasn't.

# reserved | THE SEALED READ
Then the freeze. Selection locks. Only then do we open the reserved five hundred.
The reference: @r475:*{475|four hundred seventy five}* of five hundred.
The first winner: @r478:*{478|four hundred seventy eight}* of five hundred.
About *ninety-five percent* either way. So we're calling it what it is: *no loss*, not a win.

# honest | TWO FOOTNOTES
A smaller loss in the weights is not a better robot on the task.
And held-out action error didn't rank the winners either.
Only the robot decides.

# close | WHERE WE STOOD
So: a released policy, cut to about *one sixth* of its size.
Same states. Same seeds. Same answer.
When the search finishes, the winners get their own five hundred. The report tells you which is which.
Compression you can @measure:*measure*. Or it isn't compression.
